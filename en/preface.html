1 Preface🔗

This work can be regarded as a supplement to the official documentation of the Ming language.

In the first half of 2023, for various reasons, after partially implementing the Ming language, I reflected on, organized, and summarized the motivations behind its implementation and the considerations that had led me to undertake it.

The original title was "A Contemporary Feasibility Implementation of a Chinese Programming Language: A Chinese Programming Language Based on LISP Syntax." The original work was written in DOC and PDF formats.

After completing and submitting the original document, I converted it into Racket’s Scribble document format to facilitate future maintenance.

I subsequently revised the title because I felt that the work had consistently revolved around the question of the significance of imposing Chinese on the Lisp programming language. The implementation of the Ming language was, by and large, motivated by the same question.

To facilitate the creation of a source code repository, I also gave the work an English title and acronym: "SOICOL," short for "Significance Of Imposing Chinese On Lisp."

I may later extract key parts of this work and post them on the official Racket English-language forum for discussion, gathering feedback and making further improvements and adjustments.

Source code: https://github.com/yanyingwang/SOICOL
